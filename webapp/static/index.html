COP

Columns: coil temperature. Rows: room temperature. Values are interpolated; above the table and outside the room range they are held at the edge, below the coldest coil temperature the COP drops 4 % per °C (cooling capacity falls with evaporating temperature).

Frost & defrost

Below 0 °C the water freezes on the coil and its effectiveness drops. The unit defrosts (compressor off, fan on) when the coil stays below the start temperature for the delay, until it is back above the end temperature.
